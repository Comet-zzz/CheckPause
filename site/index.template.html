Modules

六个模块，一套流程。Six modules. One workflow.

01

复盘Review

PGN 导入，Stockfish 本地逐手评估，准确度评级；AI 讲解与追问作为可选增强。PGN import, local Stockfish evaluation and accuracy rating; AI notes and follow-ups as an optional layer.

02

对弈Play

100–3000 难度的人机对局；计时、执白/执黑、开局与残局。Human-vs-engine from 100 to 3000; clocks, colours, openings and endgames.

03

谜题Puzzles

Lichess 题集与自定义导入；提示、重试、按难度和主题。Lichess sets and custom imports; hints, retries, difficulty and themes.

04

学习Lessons

从棋子走法到基础将杀的分步课程与练习。Step-by-step lessons from piece moves to basic mates.

05

统计Stats

对局数、平均准确度、最近表现与历史记录。Games played, average accuracy, recent form and history.

06

个性化Themes

10 套棋子、6 种棋盘配色、明暗主题与音效。10 piece sets, 6 board colours, light/dark themes and sound.
